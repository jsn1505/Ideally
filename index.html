      Terms and privacy

      Terms of play

      • Ideally is a free game made for fun. It comes as it is, with no guarantees, and it can change, reset or close at any time.
      • Coins, houses, items and everything else in the game have no real-world value and can't be exchanged for money.
      • Be kind. No harassment, hate, threats, sexual content, spam, or sharing anyone's personal details.
      • In-game crime, raids and votes are part of the game, not a licence to bully real people.
      • Don't cheat, exploit bugs or attack the game's servers. Report bugs instead.
      • Only add safe, legal links to the library. Links lead to other websites that we don't control.
      • We may remove messages, articles or accounts that break these rules.

      Privacy

      • If you give us your email, its only use is to connect your account across devices: we send you a log-in link so you can play anywhere. No newsletters, no marketing, and it is never shared or sold.
      • To run the game we also keep your player name, your progress, and the chat messages and articles you post. Other players can see your name, chats and articles.
      • Everything is stored with Supabase, the database the game runs on.
      • Want your account deleted? Ask the game's owner and we'll remove it.
